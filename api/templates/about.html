About Me

Are you eager to hear a story of transformation?

Allow me to introduce myself - I'm "Charles", an aspiring Data Scientist. However, my journey to this career has been anything but ordinary.

For over a decade, I worked as a Biomedical Researcher. But as time went by I became enthralled with Big Data and how it can be used to increase organisational efficiency and profitability.

I unearthed a passion for problem-solving, logical thinking, and the possibilities of data and AI.

So, I bravely chose to take a leap of faith and transition into a new career in Data Science. It has been one of the most rewarding and challenging periods in my career, and wouldn't want it any other way.

Now, I'm on a mission to create solutions that make a difference, utilizing my skills and expertise in Data Science.

Feel free to explore my projects and blog to see what I'm up to. 👨‍💻
